
# DEM CASH INTERNATIONAL

**Your Money. Your World. Your Future.**

DEM CASH INTERNATIONAL is a professional digital financial services platform, starting in Sierra Leone, with plans for international expansion.

## Our Vision
To make sending, receiving, transferring and managing money easier, safer and more accessible.

## Payment Services
- Mobile Money
- Bank Transfers
- Cash Deposits
- Debit Card Payments
- International Money Transfers
- Cryptocurrency (separate feature)

## Platform Features
- Secure customer registration and login
- Personal customer accounts
- Money transfer services
- Transaction history
- Account balance management
- Customer support
- Administrative dashboard
